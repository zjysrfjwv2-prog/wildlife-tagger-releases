# Reminder: Artist mockups must match the artist's style

**Applies to:** the Artist website system, the Artist notebook system, emails sent to artists, and all generated mockups.

## The rule

Every mockup we generate must place the art in a setting that fits the artist's work. The room, scene, décor, and mood should reflect the style and subject of the art.

## Example

- **Jason Nelson's** art should **not** appear in a nursery. Pick rooms and scenes that suit his work instead.

## What to check before a mockup goes out

- [ ] The room type fits the art (e.g. no nurseries or kids' rooms for art that doesn't suit them).
- [ ] Furniture, colors, and décor complement the art's style and palette.
- [ ] The overall mood of the scene matches the mood of the art.
- [ ] The same standard applies everywhere mockups appear: website, notebook, and artist emails.

## Implementation idea

Store a style profile per artist (subject, mood, palette, suitable rooms, rooms to avoid) and use it to pick or prompt mockup scenes, so this happens automatically rather than by hand.
